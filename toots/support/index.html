Support

Something not working? Here's how to fix it.

How do I make it fart when I unfold?

Fold your iPhone, open Toots on the outer screen, then unfold. The app has to be open when you unfold, because iOS doesn't let apps react to the hinge while they're closed.

How does Incognito mode work?

Tap Incognito mode, choose any picture (a screenshot of your Home Screen works great), and turn on the switch. The picture covers the screen so nobody knows Toots is open, and unfolding still farts. To get out, hold your finger on the screen for 2 seconds.

I don't hear anything.

Turn the volume up with the side buttons. Toots plays even when your iPhone is on silent. If you have headphones or a Bluetooth speaker connected, the sound goes there instead.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Toots, scroll to Your fart, and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Recording doesn't work.

Toots needs microphone access. Go to Settings › Toots and turn on Microphone.

How do I delete my recording?

In Toots, tap Delete under Your fart. Your recording only ever exists on your iPhone, so once it's deleted, it's gone.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.
