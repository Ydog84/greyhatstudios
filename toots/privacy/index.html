Privacy Policy

Updated October 31, 2026

The short version

Toots doesn't collect any data about you. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your recordings and pictures stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded anywhere.

Microphone and recordings

Toots asks for microphone access only when you choose to record. The microphone is used for two features:

  • Record your own fart (a paid feature): your recording is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. It's never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it in the app at any time, and deleting the app removes it too.
  • Fart Identifier: your recording is analyzed on your iPhone and then deleted right away. It's never saved or sent anywhere.

You can turn microphone access off at any time in Settings › Toots › Microphone.

Photos

Incognito mode lets you choose one picture to cover the screen. Toots uses Apple's photo picker, so it never gets access to your photo library. It only receives the single picture you choose. That picture is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app, and is never sent anywhere. Choosing a different picture replaces it, and deleting the app removes it.

Data stored on your device

Toots saves a few things on your iPhone so the app works, like your sound setting. This stays on your device, and we can't see it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy covers these: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Toots doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
